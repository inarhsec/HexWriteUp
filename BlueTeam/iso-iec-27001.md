## Example

Create a clean, professional, high-resolution mind map with a white background.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 "Information Security Management System (ISMS)"

Use blue, green, and orange colors for major branches.

The center should branch into the following main sections.

***

1. Context of the Organization (Clause 4)

***

Include branches:

• Understanding the Organization • Interested Parties • Scope of ISMS • ISMS Processes

Purpose: Understand the business before implementing security.

Reminder: "Know what must be protected."

Practical GRC Example:

* Define scope for Banking Applications
* Identify regulators (RBI, NPCI)
* Identify customers, vendors, auditors
* Include data center and cloud environments

Relevant Annex A: • A.5 Organizational Controls • A.5.19 Information Security in Supplier Relationships

***

2. Leadership (Clause 5)

***

Branches:

• Leadership Commitment • Information Security Policy • Roles & Responsibilities

Purpose: Top management drives ISMS.

Reminder: "No management support = No ISMS."

Practical Example:

* Security Policy approval
* Assign CISO
* Define Asset Owners
* Management Review Meetings

Relevant Annex A: • A.5.1 Policies • A.5.2 Information Security Roles • A.5.4 Management Responsibilities

***

3. Planning (Clause 6)

***

Branches:

• Risks • Opportunities • Information Security Objectives • Risk Treatment Plan • Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Purpose: Plan security before implementing controls.

Reminder: "Identify → Assess → Treat."

Practical Example:

* Perform Risk Assessment
* Risk Register
* Vendor Risk Assessment
* Vulnerability Risk Rating
* Select Annex A controls
* Create SoA

Connect Annex A controls:

• A.5 Organizational Controls • A.8 Asset Management • A.9 Access Control • A.12 Operations Security • A.13 Network Security

Show A.9 Access Control connected to Risk Treatment.

***

4. Support (Clause 7)

***

Branches:

• Resources • Competence • Awareness • Communication • Documented Information

Purpose: Support the ISMS.

Reminder: "People + Documentation."

Practical Example:

* Security Awareness Training
* ISO Training
* Policies
* SOPs
* Asset Inventory
* Audit Evidence
* Record Management

Relevant Annex A:

• A.6 People Controls • A.7 Physical Controls • A.8 Technology Controls

***

5. Operation (Clause 8)

***

Branches:

• Operational Planning • Risk Assessment • Risk Treatment • Implement Controls

Purpose: Run the ISMS.

Reminder: "Do what you planned."

Practical Example:

* Firewall Rule Review
* Server Hardening
* Backup Monitoring
* Vulnerability Management
* Patch Management
* Incident Handling
* Vendor Security Review

Connect Annex A:

• A.8 Asset Management • A.9 Access Control • A.12 Operations Security • A.13 Communications Security • A.16 Incident Management • A.17 Business Continuity

***

6. Performance Evaluation (Clause 9)

***

Branches:

• Monitoring • Measurement • Internal Audit • Management Review • KPIs

Purpose: Measure ISMS performance.

Reminder: "If you can't measure it, you can't improve it."

Practical Example:

* KPI Dashboard
* Audit Findings
* Compliance Reports
* Vulnerability Trends
* Backup Success Rate
* Patch Compliance
* Firewall Rule Audit Reports
* SIEM Reports

Relevant Annex A: Supports all implemented controls through monitoring.

***

7. Improvement (Clause 10)

***

Branches:

• Nonconformity • Corrective Action • Continual Improvement

Purpose: Improve the ISMS.

Reminder: "Find → Fix → Improve."

Practical Example:

* RBI Audit Observation
* NPCI Observation
* Root Cause Analysis
* Corrective Action Plan
* Preventive Improvements

Relevant Annex A: Continuous improvement of every implemented control.
